About This Game

A tiny 3D toy-store cleanup sim where every misplaced plushie, dragon, robot, board game, and block set belongs in the right wall, bin, pegboard, shelf, or table before sunrise. Some toys are broken into separate pieces and must be fixed at the repair bench before they can go back on display. Completed displays earn tool credits for shop equipment like the scanner.

Controls

  • WASDMove through the shop in first person
  • Mouse / ArrowsLook around, including up and down
  • Click ViewLock mouse look; Tab or Esc releases it
  • E / SpacePick up, shelf, place on the bench, or place on the floor
  • GQuick-drop the active toy
  • TOpen or close the shop tools screen
  • Ctrl+S / Ctrl+LSave / load cleanup progress
